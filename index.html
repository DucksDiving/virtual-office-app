Virtual Office

An internal office assistant for Ducks Underwater Specialties

Virtual Office is a private tool that Ducks Underwater Specialties, a marine construction business, runs on its own office computer. It helps the owners keep up with day-to-day office work, such as finding and summarizing the business's own email when an owner asks.

Who can use it

Only the business itself. Virtual Office is not a public product, has no sign-up, and is not offered to customers or anyone outside the business. The only Google account it connects to is the business's own.

What it does with Google

With the business's permission, Virtual Office reads the business's own Gmail mailbox. Its access is read-only: it cannot send, change or delete email.
