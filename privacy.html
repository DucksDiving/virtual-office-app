Privacy Policy

Virtual Office · Effective November 20, 2026

Virtual Office is an internal tool operated by Ducks Underwater Specialties ("the business") for its own use. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Google account data the tool accesses and what happens to it.

Who this applies to

The only Google account connected to Virtual Office is the business's own account. The tool does not collect information from website visitors, and this site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

What is accessed

With the business's consent, Virtual Office has read-only access to the business's Gmail mailbox (messages, labels and settings). It cannot send, modify or delete email. It also sees the account's email address, to confirm it is connected to the correct mailbox.

How it is used

Email is read only to carry out office tasks that the owners of the business ask for, such as finding a message or summarizing recent mail. It is not used for advertising and is never sold.

Where it is kept

The tool runs on a computer owned by the business. The Google sign-in credential is stored in that computer's secure keychain. The tool does not copy the mailbox to any other server or database.

Sharing

To carry out a requested task, relevant email content may be processed by the AI service provider the business uses to run its assistant (currently Anthropic). It is shared with no one else, except where the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Virtual Office's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The business can remove Virtual Office's access at any time from its Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access also makes the stored sign-in credential useless.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be directed to Ducks Underwater Specialties.
